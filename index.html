About Us

Crusty Crumbs is a dedicated food manufacturing company based in the heart of Lagos, Nigeria. Operating from our facility in Omole Phase 2, we combine traditional baking techniques with modern food safety standards to produce consistently excellent baked goods. Our commitment to quality ingredients, hygienic production processes, and customer satisfaction has made us a preferred choice for individuals, restaurants, hotels, and retailers across Lagos. Whether you need daily bread supplies, special occasion cakes, or bulk orders for your business, Crusty Crumbs delivers freshness and flavour you can trust.
